Prototyping and Communication

Throughout the semester, you have created various standard artifacts which are typical for the product development lifecycle.

In many cases, a prototype can be the most useful. Remember that the "customer doesn't always know what they want until they see what they don't want." A Prototype is a realistic representation. It gives internal and external stakeholders a better view of the direction and plan for a project. It is equally valuable to the development team.

Communication isn't just with the customer, often these collective artifacts help clarify ideas and directions within the design group as well, presenting opportunities, new ideas, and even limitations. Sometimes an idea for a future enhancement proves to be beyond the scope of budget or schedule for the next release and needs to be descoped, or an idea may take the design in another direction.

It is my hope that developing a prototype was revealing in some ways to guide the next phases of development.
